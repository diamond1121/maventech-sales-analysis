# MavenTech Sales Performance and Business Analysis

## Project Overview
This project analyses MavenTech's sales pipeline to evaluate sales performance, identify business trends, and provide data-driven recommendations to support management decisions.

## Tools Used
- MySQL
- SQL

## Project Objectives
- Evaluate sales performance across different sales agents.
- Analyse product revenue and deal performance.
- Compare regional sales performance.
- Examine customer and industry revenue contributions.
- Identify opportunities for improving sales conversion.

## Key Findings
- The overall win rate among completed deals (Won and Lost) was **63.15%**.
- Darcel Schlecht recorded the highest sales revenue among the sales agents.
- GTXPro generated the highest product revenue.
- The West region recorded the highest total revenue.
- Retail contributed the highest revenue among the industry sectors.
- Prospecting activity was relatively low, with 500 opportunities recorded.

## Project Files
- `maventech business analysis.pdf` – Complete project report.
- `maventech analysis(2).sql` – SQL queries used for the analysis.

## Recommendations
- Strengthen prospecting activities to increase the sales pipeline.
- Study the strategies of high-performing sales agents.
- Investigate products with low revenue contributions.
- Develop strategies to improve sales conversion.
- Build on the performance of strong regional and industry segments.

## Project Limitations
The dataset does not contain product cost information. Therefore, revenue figures cannot be used to establish product profitability.

## Conclusion
This project demonstrates the use of SQL to analyse business data, identify performance trends, and communicate findings that can support informed business decisions.
