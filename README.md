# MavenTech Sales Performance and Business Analysis

## Project Overview
This project uses SQL to analyse MavenTech's sales pipeline, evaluate sales performance, identify business trends, and provide data-driven recommendations to support management decisions.

## Tools Used
- MySQL
- SQL

## Project Objectives
- Evaluate sales performance across different sales agents.
- Analyse product revenue and deal performance.
- Compare regional sales performance.
- Examine customer and industry revenue contributions.
- Identify opportunities for improving sales conversion.

## Key Findings

1. **Sales Win Rate:** The win rate among completed deals (Won and Lost) was 63.15%.
2. **Top Sales Agent:** Darcel Schlecht recorded the highest sales revenue.
3. **Top Product:** GTXPro generated the highest product revenue.
4. **Regional Performance:** The West region recorded the highest total revenue.
5. **Industry Performance:** Retail contributed the highest revenue among industry sectors.
6. **Prospecting:** Prospecting activity was relatively low, with only 500 opportunities recorded.

## Business Recommendations

- Strengthen prospecting activities to increase the sales pipeline.
- Study and adopt strategies used by high-performing sales agents.
- Investigate products with low revenue contributions.
- Develop strategies to improve sales conversion.
- Build on the performance of strong regional and industry segments.

## Project Limitations

The dataset does not contain product cost information. Therefore, revenue figures cannot be used to establish product profitability.

## Conclusion

This project demonstrates the application of SQL in analysing business data, identifying performance trends, and communicating findings that can support informed business decisions.
